Make a course document accessible

Upload a Word or PDF file. This page checks it against the WCAG 2.1 AA items that matter most for course documents, fixes what it can, and hands you a corrected copy.

Or drop it on this page. Your file stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

What this tool cannot judge for you
  • Whether alt text is accurate. Suggestions are a starting point; you know what each image is teaching.
  • Math that exists only as a picture. Alt text makes it readable, but retyping problems with Word's Equation editor (Insert, then Equation) is the most accessible form, because screen readers can step through each term.
  • Reading order of floating pictures and text boxes, meaning carried by color alone, and videos (which need captions).
  • Content inside headers, footers, and comments.

After downloading, open the file in Word and run Review, then Check Accessibility, for a final pass. When you need a PDF, use File, then Save As, PDF, and keep "Document structure tags for accessibility" turned on.
