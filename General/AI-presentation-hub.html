Beyond the Chatbot

Using AI to build better learning experiences: from course communication and practice to active learning, career relevance, and student success.

Faculty-facing AII use AI behind the scenes to build announcements, webpages, practice, calculators, and activities. Students never have to touch an AI tool.
Student-facing AIStudents use AI as part of learning, which raises questions about prompting, verification, critical thinking, and academic integrity.

Using AI in teaching does not necessarily mean asking students to use AI. Every example on this page is faculty-facing.

Every example follows the same path. AI isn't the goal; student learning is.
