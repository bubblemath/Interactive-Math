Activity 1 · Statistics

Which Tool Is This?

Students decide: which statistical tool fits the situation?

Students read short real-world scenarios and pick the right method with no arithmetic, and the page tracks which tools cause their mistakes.

 Activity 2 · Fractions

The Denominator Ladder

Students decide: do the denominators need to match, can I cancel, which fraction flips?

Before doing any arithmetic, students choose the right setup for each fraction operation across 11 rungs, until picking the rule becomes automatic.

 Activity 3 · Sets

The Venn Reading Ladder

Students decide: which of the four regions does each element belong in?

Over 10 rungs, students name regions, read sets and counts from the picture (including outside both circles), and translate between diagrams and lists.

 Activity 4 · Linear equations

The Form Ladder

Students decide: is standard form or slope-intercept form better for this question?

Across 11 rungs, students match the equation's form to what the question asks, read answers straight from that form, and convert only when needed.
