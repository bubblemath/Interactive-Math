Skill 5.1: Ratios, Rates & Unit Rates

Goal: write ratios in simplest form with matching units, and find unit rates by dividing.

Unit-rate builder and ratio-or-rate sorter

Type a rate. The bar splits it into equal parts, one for each unit on the bottom, so you can see the unit rate. Then sort comparisons into ratios and rates.

for

Type "dollars" as the top unit for money. The bottom amount can be a decimal like 8.5.

Ratio or rate?

A ratio compares quantities with the same units. A rate compares different units. Sort each one.

    Questions to explore

    Try 120 miles in 4 hours, then press "Flip it." Which unit rate answers "How fast?"

    30 miles per hour answers "how fast." The flipped rate, 0.033 hours per mile, answers "how long does one mile take?" Put the quantity named first ("miles per hour") on top.

    Try $95 for 8.5 hours. Why is the last part of the bar only half as wide?

    8.5 hours is 8 full hours and half of another. Each full hour earns $95 ÷ 8.5 ≈ $11.18, and the half hour earns half of that. Round to the nearest cent only at the end.

    In the sorter, why is "3 feet to 8 inches" a ratio but not 3/8?

    Feet and inches both measure length, so it is a ratio, but the units must match before you simplify. 3 feet = 36 inches, and 36/8 = 9/2. Writing 3/8 would compare feet to inches.

    What does the word "per" tell you?

    "Per" means "for each one." 60 miles per hour is 60 miles for each 1 hour, so the bottom of a unit rate is always 1.
