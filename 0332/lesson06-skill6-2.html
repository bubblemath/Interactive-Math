Skill 6.2: The Distributive Property

Goal: distribute a number, a negative number, or a minus sign over every term in parentheses.

Area model and spot the mistake

Distributing is finding the area of a rectangle split into parts: the number outside is the height, and each term inside is one part's width. Press Next to multiply one part at a time.

Type a number times parentheses, like −2(5x + 4), −(x − 3), or (3x − 2)5.

    Spot the mistake

    Click the term that is wrong, or press "It's correct."

    Questions to explore

    Try 6(100 − 1). How does the distributive property help with mental math?

    6 · 99 is hard, but 6 · 100 − 6 · 1 = 600 − 6 = 594 is easy. Splitting a number into friendly parts and distributing is the same property.

    Try −2(5x + 4). Why is the second part −8 and not +8?

    The −2 multiplies every term, sign and all: −2 · 4 = −8. A negative outside changes the sign of every term inside.

    Try −(−2x + 9y − 7). Where is the number outside?

    A minus sign in front of parentheses means −1. Multiplying each term by −1 flips every sign: 2x − 9y + 7.

    Try 500(1 + 0.04t). Where will you see this?

    It is the simple-interest formula A = P(1 + rt) with P = $500 and r = 4%. Distributing gives A = 500 + 20t: the $500 you start with plus $20 of interest each year. You will use it in Math 1332 Lesson 11.
