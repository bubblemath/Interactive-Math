Skill 11.2: Cube Roots & Negative Radicands

Goal: evaluate cube, fourth and fifth roots, and decide when a root of a negative is not a real number.

Sign sorter

Questions to explore

Try √(−16). Why does no whole number work, positive or negative?

4² = 16 and (−4)² = 16. Squaring a negative number makes it positive, so no real number squared gives −16. √(−16) is not a real number.

Try ∛(−27). Why does a negative answer work this time?

Three negative factors make a negative: (−3)(−3)(−3) = −27. So ∛(−27) = −3. Every odd root of a negative number is negative.

Compare ∜(−16) with ⁵√(−32). What decides whether a negative radicand works?

The index. An even index (2, 4, 6, …) of a negative is not real; an odd index (3, 5, …) of a negative is negative.

Type −√49 into your head and compare it with √(−49). What is the difference?

−√49 takes the root first, then makes it negative: −7. √(−49) asks for a number whose square is −49, and there is no real one.
