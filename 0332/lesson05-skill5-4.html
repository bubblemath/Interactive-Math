Skill 5.4: Percent Equations

Goal: translate percent sentences into equations and solve for the part, the whole, or the percent.

Percent bar and sentence translator

The percent bar lines up percents (top) with amounts (bottom): 100% sits under the whole. The translator turns a percent sentence into an equation one word at a time.

%

    Sentence translator

    "Of" means ×, "is" means =, and "what" is the unknown x. Type a sentence or pick one.

      Questions to explore

      Try Julia's class. Can you find 20% of 45 without the equation?

      Yes. 10% of 45 is 4.5 (move the point one place left), so 20% is twice that: 9 students. The bar shows 20% lining up with 9.

      Try Pablo's exam. Why is the whole bigger than 27?

      27 questions is only 90% of the exam, so the whole must be a little more than 27. 0.90 · x = 27 gives x = 30. If your answer for the whole is smaller than the part (with a percent under 100%), check your setup.

      In the translator, which number follows the word "of"?

      The whole. "12 is 30% of what number" means 12 = 0.30 × x, so the unknown is the whole. In "38% of 200," the whole is 200.

      Is there another way to set up a percent problem?

      Yes, as a proportion: part/whole = percent/100. For the acid, 35/60 = x/100, so 60x = 3,500 and x ≈ 58.3%. Both methods give the same answer.
