Unit 1: Algebra Foundations & Functions

Unit goal: build the algebra toolkit (expressions, equations, inequalities), then use it to work with functions: domains, graphs, transformations, combinations, and inverses.

Unit 1 has six lessons. Each lesson works the same way:

  1. Lesson hub: the lesson's objective, a before-class checklist, and links to its skills.
  2. Skill pages: Explore, Examples, and Practice for one skill at a time.
  3. Ladder: a short check of every step in the lesson. It shows what to review.

    Getting ready for the Unit 1 exam

    A study plan

    1. Climb each lesson's ladder with 4 questions per rung, the hardest setting.
    2. For every rung marked Shaky or Needs work, open the skill page in your review list. Work through the Examples, then the Practice.
    3. Climb that ladder again. When every rung is cleared, move to the next lesson.
    4. Finish with the practice problems in your student notes, without looking at the examples.
