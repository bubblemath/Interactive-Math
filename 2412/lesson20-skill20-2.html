Skill 20.2: Algebra with Trig Expressions

Goal: factor, combine fractions, and multiply by conjugates with trigonometric expressions, then finish with an identity.

The same algebra, twice

Each trig problem below is an algebra problem in disguise. Step through both versions side by side.

Questions to explore

In the conjugate problems, why multiply by the sum when the bottom is a difference?

(a − b)(a + b) = a² − b². With a = 1 and b = cos θ, the bottom becomes 1 − cos²θ, which a Pythagorean identity turns into one term, sin²θ.

Why can't you cancel the sin θ in (sin θ + cos θ)/(sin θ)?

You can cancel only factors, not terms. Split the fraction instead: (sin θ)/(sin θ) + (cos θ)/(sin θ) = 1 + cot θ.

Where does a Pythagorean identity show up at the end of each problem?

Whenever sin²θ + cos²θ, 1 − sin²θ, or 1 − cos²θ appears, replace it with a single term.
