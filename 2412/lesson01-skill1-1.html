Skill 1.1: Polynomial Operations & Special Products

Goal: add, subtract, and multiply polynomials, and use the special product formulas to expand quickly.

Use ^ for powers: x^2 + 3x − 1.

Result

Check with a number

Two expressions that are equal give the same value for every x.

Questions to explore

Is (x + 3)² the same as x² + 9? Use the number check with x = 1.

No. (1 + 3)² = 16, but 1² + 9 = 10. Squaring a sum makes four boxes, and the two middle boxes (3x and 3x) give the missing 6x. Pick the preset (2x + 3)² to see them.

Try (x + 5)(x − 5). What happens to the middle boxes?

They are +5x and −5x, so they cancel. That is why (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b² has no middle term.

How many boxes does a trinomial times a trinomial make? How many terms are left after combining?

3 × 3 = 9 boxes. Boxes on the same diagonal have the same power, so they combine, leaving at most 5 terms (powers 4, 3, 2, 1, 0).

Switch to A³ with A = 2x − 1. Where do the coefficients 8, −12, 6, −1 come from?

From (a − b)³ = a³ − 3a²b + 3ab² − b³ with a = 2x and b = 1: (2x)³ = 8x³, 3(2x)²(1) = 12x², 3(2x)(1)² = 6x, and 1³ = 1.
