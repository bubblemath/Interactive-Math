Lesson 1: Algebraic & Rational Expressions

Lesson objective: simplify, combine, and factor polynomial and rational expressions, and rationalize denominators.

This lesson has five skills. Each skill page has three parts: Explore (hands-on tools), Examples (as many step-by-step examples as you need), and Practice (eight questions with hints; type your own answers for most of them).

The big idea

(x² − 9)/(x² + 4x + 3) = ((x − 3)(x + 3))/((x + 1)(x + 3))

Factor firstAlmost every skill in this lesson starts by factoring.
Cancel factors, not termsOnly things multiplied by everything can cancel.
