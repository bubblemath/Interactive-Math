Math 2412 Precalculus

Course goal: build the algebra, functions, and trigonometry you need for calculus: equations and inequalities, polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions, the trigonometric functions and their identities, the conic sections, and sequences, series, and the Binomial Theorem.

The course has four units and 26 lessons. Each lesson works the same way:

  1. Lesson hub: the objective, a before-class checklist, and links to the skills.
  2. Skill pages: Explore, Examples, and Practice for one skill at a time.
  3. Ladder: a short check of every step in the lesson. It shows what to review.

Each unit ends with a unit exam, and the course ends with a comprehensive final exam.
