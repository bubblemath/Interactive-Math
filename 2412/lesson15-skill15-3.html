Skill 15.3: The Six Trigonometric Functions

Goal: find all six trigonometric functions from a point on the unit circle or at a special angle, know where each is undefined, and use even and odd properties.

Six functions from one point

If the terminal side of t meets the unit circle at (x, y): sin t = y, cos t = x, tan t = y/x, csc t = 1/y, sec t = 1/x, cot t = x/y.

Questions to explore

Why is tan(π/2) undefined? Which other functions are undefined somewhere?

At π/2 the point is (0, 1), and tan = y/x divides by 0. Tangent and secant are undefined where x = 0; cotangent and cosecant where y = 0.

Why is cosine even?

The angle −t is the reflection of t across the x-axis. Reflecting keeps x and flips the sign of y, so cos(−t) = cos t and sin(−t) = −sin t.

How do you pair the reciprocals without mixing them up?

Each pair has exactly one "co": sine with cosecant, cosine with secant, tangent with cotangent.
