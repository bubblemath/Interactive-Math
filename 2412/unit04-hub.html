Unit 4: Analytic Trigonometry, Conics & Discrete Topics

Unit goal: verify identities and use the sum, difference, double-angle, and half-angle formulas, solve trigonometric equations, graph and write equations of parabolas, ellipses, and hyperbolas, and work with sequences, series, and the Binomial Theorem.

Unit 4 has seven lessons. Each lesson works the same way:

  1. Lesson hub: the lesson's objective, a before-class checklist, and links to its skills.
  2. Skill pages: Explore, Examples, and Practice for one skill at a time.
  3. Ladder: a short check of every step in the lesson. It shows what to review.

Lessons

Lessons 20–22 cover identities, the sum and difference formulas, and trig equations. Lessons 23–24 cover the conic sections. Lessons 25–26 cover sequences, series, and the Binomial Theorem.

    Getting ready for the Unit 4 exam

    A study plan

    1. Climb each lesson's ladder with 4 questions per rung, the hardest setting.
    2. For every rung marked Shaky or Needs work, open the skill page in your review list. Work through the Examples, then the Practice.
    3. Climb that ladder again. When every rung is cleared, move to the next lesson.
    4. Finish with the practice problems in your student notes, without looking at the examples. Keep a list of the identities and the conic standard forms, and bring a calculator for Lesson 22.
