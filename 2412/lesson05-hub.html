Lesson 5: Transformations & Symmetry

Lesson objective: graph functions by applying shifts, stretches, compressions, and reflections to parent functions, and classify functions as even, odd, or neither.

This lesson has five skills. Each skill page has three parts: Explore (move sliders and spot patterns), Examples (as many step-by-step examples as you need), and Practice (eight questions with hints).

The big idea

y = a·f(b(x − h)) + k

Inside: b and hchange x-values, and work backward.
x → x ÷ b + h
Outside: a and kchange y-values, as you'd expect.
y → a·y + k

Mapping rule: (x, y) → (x ÷ b + h, a·y + k)
