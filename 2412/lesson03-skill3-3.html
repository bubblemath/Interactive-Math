Skill 3.3: Polynomial & Rational Inequalities (Sign Charts)

Goal: solve polynomial and rational inequalities with a sign chart, including the endpoints correctly.

Sign chart builder

Each factor changes sign only at its zero. Click each box to choose + or −. (Tip: in each column, test any number in that interval.) Then the chart multiplies the signs and shades the intervals you need.

Questions to explore

Why can't you multiply both sides of (x + 1)/(x − 4) ≥ 0 by x − 4?

You don't know whether x − 4 is positive or negative, so you don't know whether to flip the sign. The sign chart avoids the problem.

Choose "Squared factor." Why doesn't the sign change at the repeated zero?

A squared factor like (x − 1)² is positive on both sides of 1, so it never flips the product's sign there.

Why does a zero of the denominator always get a parenthesis, even with ≥?

The expression is undefined there, so that x can never be part of the solution.
