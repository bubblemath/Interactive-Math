Cooling Fin Explorer

A fin only works if heat can travel out to its tip faster than the air strips it away. Change the metal, the air, and the shape, and watch that competition play out.

The fin is drawn in side view, coloured by temperature, sitting directly above its own temperature curve — both share the same horizontal axis, so the colour you see and the curve below it are the same information twice.
