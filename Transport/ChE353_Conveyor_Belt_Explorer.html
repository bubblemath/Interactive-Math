Conveyor Belt Film Explorer

The belt drags oil up the incline; gravity pulls it back down. Those two effects fight inside the film, and the winner changes with depth — which is exactly what the velocity profile is telling you.

Each arrow is the oil velocity at that depth. Thicken the film and watch the outer arrows shrink, stop, and turn around while the ones near the belt still march up the incline. The curve through the arrow tips is the profile Vz(x).

Companion to the ChE 353 Topic 10 study guide. The model matches the lecture: steady, laminar, fully developed flow of a film of uniform thickness a on a belt inclined at θ from the horizontal, with no-slip at the belt and zero shear at the oil–air surface. Belt width is fixed at W = 1.5 m for the flow-rate figures. Setting U = 0 turns the belt off and reduces the problem to the falling film of BSL §2.2.
