Straight lines here mean clean power laws — the slopes are −2, −1 and 0.

What to notice. All three curves rise together inside the core, then split the moment generation stops. The sphere sheds flux fastest because its area grows as r², the cylinder as r, and the slab not at all. This is why a sphere is such an effective shape for spreading heat out — and why the cladding on the pellet in the next tab is so effective despite being thin.

The full Lecture 3 problem. Fuel generates heat non-uniformly out to R⁽ᶠ⁾, cladding generates none out to R⁽ᶜ⁾, and the outer surface is held at T₀. The colour of the cross-section is temperature, so you can see the hot centre directly.
