    Questions to explore

    Shade 25 squares. Why is it 25%, 0.25 and 1/4 all at once?

    25 out of 100 squares is 25 per hundred, which is 25%. As a decimal that is 25 hundredths, 0.25. And 25/100 simplifies to 1/4: a quarter of the grid.

    Convert 0.025. Why is it 2.5% and not 25%?

    Move the decimal point two places right: 0.025 → 2.5. A percent less than 1% or a decimal less than 0.01 is easy to misread, so count the two places carefully.

    Convert 250%. How can a percent be more than 100%?

    100% is one whole, so 250% is two and a half wholes: 2.5, or 5/2. A price that rises to 250% of the original is 2.5 times as much.

    Convert 4/3. Why does the percent need rounding?

    4 ÷ 3 = 1.333…, a repeating decimal. As a percent it is 133.333…%, which we usually round to the nearest tenth: 133.3%.
