Even: f(−x) = f(x)
Odd: f(−x) = −f(x)
Neither: Neither is true.

Even graphs have y-axis symmetry. Odd graphs have origin symmetry. A single pair of points can disprove symmetry, but cannot prove it for the entire graph.
