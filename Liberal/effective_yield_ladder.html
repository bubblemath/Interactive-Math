Lesson 14 · effective annual yield

The stated rate is not the rate you get.

An account advertised at 8% compounded monthly does not pay 8% over a year — it pays 8.3%, because the interest added each month starts earning too. The effective annual yield is the simple rate that would produce the same balance in one year, and it is the only number on which two accounts can honestly be compared.

✋

The higher stated rate does not always win. Comparing two accounts by the rate on the poster works only when they compound equally often, and they rarely do. A lower nominal rate compounded far more frequently can overtake a higher one compounded annually — so the rule is not “pick the bigger number”, it is work out both yields, then pick the bigger number. Rung 8 is built entirely from pairs where the poster lies.

Why it matters

One number, honestly comparable

01

It is why the figure is published

Banks must quote an effective yield precisely so two accounts can be compared without doing this arithmetic yourself.

02

Borrowing works the same way

The same calculation on a loan is the annual percentage rate, and it is what the true cost of borrowing is measured in.

03

It compresses the whole lesson
