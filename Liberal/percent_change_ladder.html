Lesson 11 · percent increase and percent decrease

Divide by where you started.

Percent change is one fraction: the amount of change on top, the original amount underneath. The subtraction is never the hard part. Choosing the denominator is — and putting the new value there instead gives a different answer every time.

✋

The same two numbers give two different answers. A sofa falling from $840 to $714 is a 15% decrease. The same sofa rising from $714 back to $840 is a 17.65% increase. The change is $126 either way — only the denominator moved. So “15% off, then 15% back on” does not return you to the start, and a headline about a percent change is meaningless until you know which number it was divided by.

Why it matters

The denominator is the claim

01

Percent changes do not cancel

Down 20% then up 20% leaves you below where you started, because the second percent is taken from a smaller number.

02

Direction changes the figure

The same gap between two numbers is a different percent depending on which end you call the original.

03

It is how every trend is reported

Sales, prices, population, turnout. Knowing what a reported percent was divided by is most of reading the news.
