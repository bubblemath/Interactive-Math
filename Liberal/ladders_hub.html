The Ladders

Every lesson in the course has two or three ladders. A ladder is one page of practice on one idea, split into ten rungs that climb from naming the parts to doing the whole thing unaided. Nothing is locked — start at rung 1, or go straight to the step that trips you up. Pick a lesson below and read what each ladder covers before you open it.

How a ladder works

The same shape every time

01

A worked demonstration first

Every ladder opens with the idea laid out in full, using the figures from your own worksheet. Six buttons step through it. Read that before you start answering.

02

Ten rungs, nothing locked

Rung 1 is the most basic skill and rung 10 mixes everything. Jump straight to whichever rung you need — you do not have to earn the ones before it.

03

Four in a row earns the badge

Answer with the mouse or press 1–4, then Enter for the next question. One miss resets the streak. Questions are generated fresh, so the numbers never repeat.

04

Every wrong answer has a reason

The three wrong options are not random. Each one is a specific mistake, and choosing it tells you which mistake you just made and where it went wrong.
