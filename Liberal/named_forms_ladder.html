Lesson 10 · the four valid forms and the four fallacies

Recognise the shape and skip the table.

Almost every argument in this course is one of eight standard shapes, and each valid one has a fallacy sitting right beside it that differs by a single line. Once you can tell the pairs apart on sight, you no longer need to build anything — the name carries the verdict.

✋

Each fallacy is its valid partner with one line changed. Direct reasoning affirms the first part of the conditional; the fallacy of the converse affirms the second. Contrapositive reasoning denies the second; the fallacy of the inverse denies the first. Disjunctive reasoning denies one side; its misuse affirms one. In every pair the two arguments look almost identical on the page, which is exactly why the wrong one is persuasive.
