Polygons: Names, Interior Angles & Classification

Name polygons by side count, compute interior angle sums and individual angles, find exterior angles, count diagonals, and classify by regularity and convexity.

💡
Interior angle sum: For any polygon with n sides, the interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°. Divide by n to get each angle of a regular polygon.
⚠️
Exterior angles always sum to 360° for any convex polygon — regardless of the number of sides. Each exterior angle of a regular n-gon = 360° ÷ n.
📐
Diagonals: A polygon with n sides has n(n − 3) / 2 diagonals. Regular = all sides & angles equal. Convex = all interior angles < 180°.
