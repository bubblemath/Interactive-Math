Ladder 12 — Circumference & Arc Length

Master the formulas for circumference and arc length. Each rung earns a badge when you answer 4 in a row correctly.

Formula Reference
FormulaEquationNotes
Circumference (radius)C = 2πrr = radius; multiply by 2π
Circumference (diameter)C = πdd = 2r; equivalent form
Arc LengthL = (θ/360°) · 2πrθ = central angle in degrees; arc is a fraction of the full circumference
Arc Length (radians)L = rθθ in radians; elegant but same result
💡
Key idea: Arc length is just a fraction of the full circumference. The fraction is θ/360°, where θ is the central angle.
⚠️
Don't confuse arc length (a distance along the circle) with arc measure (an angle in degrees). They are related but different things.
📐
Leave answers in terms of π unless told to approximate. E.g. write 12π cm not 37.7 cm.
