Ladder 22 — Area of Polygons & Circles

Area measures how much flat surface a shape covers — always expressed in square units. Each formula below describes a different way to "fill" a shape. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

Area Formulas
ShapeFormulaVariables
SquareA = s²s = side length
RectangleA = l × wl = length, w = width
ParallelogramA = b × hb = base, h = perpendicular height
TriangleA = ½ × b × hb = base, h = perpendicular height
TrapezoidA = ½ × (b₁ + b₂) × hb₁, b₂ = parallel bases, h = height
CircleA = πr²r = radius
SemicircleA = ½πr²Half the circle's area
💡
Square units, always. Area is always in square units (ft², cm², in²…). If your answer doesn't say "square ___", something is missing.
⚠️
Height ≠ slant side. For triangles, parallelograms, and trapezoids the height must be perpendicular to the base — not the slant side length.
📐
Composite figures. Split an irregular shape into rectangles, triangles, or circles. Add areas of pieces you include; subtract pieces you cut out.
