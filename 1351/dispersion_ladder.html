Range, IQR, Variance & Standard Deviation

Ten rungs from computing each spread measure step-by-step to interpreting what standard deviation tells us about a data set.

Range
max − min
Simplest spread measure. Very sensitive to outliers — only uses two values.
IQR
Q3 − Q1
Spread of the middle 50%. Resistant to outliers. Used in box plots.
Variance (s²)
Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n−1)
Average squared deviation from mean. Uses n−1 (sample) for unbiased estimate.
Std Dev (s)
s = √(variance)
Square root of variance. Same units as the data. Most-used spread measure.
⚠️
Divide by n − 1: Sample variance uses n − 1 (degrees of freedom), not n. Dividing by n underestimates the true population variance.
📌
IQR method: Q1 = median of the lower half, Q3 = median of the upper half (exclude the overall median when n is odd).
💡
Variance vs. SD: Variance is in squared units (e.g., inches²), so we take the square root to get standard deviation back in the original units.
