Ladder 16 — Rotations

Spin every point around the origin by a fixed angle. Master the four coordinate rules, apply them to triangles, identify a rotation from its effect, and reason about what rotations preserve. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

Rotation Rules about the Origin
RotationRule: (x, y) →Memory trick
90° CCW (= 270° CW)(−y, x)Swap, negate the new x
180° (CW or CCW)(−x, −y)Negate both
270° CCW (= 90° CW)(y, −x)Swap, negate the new y
360° (any direction)(x, y)Full turn — back to start
💡
CCW is the default. In geometry, positive angles rotate counter-clockwise (CCW). Clockwise (CW) rotations use negative angles or equivalents: 90° CW = 270° CCW.
⚠️
Swap then sign. For 90° CCW: swap x and y first, then negate the first coordinate. Getting the order wrong is the #1 mistake.
📐
Same equivalence, different name. 90° CW and 270° CCW land on the same point. Always check whether the problem uses CW or CCW before applying a rule.
