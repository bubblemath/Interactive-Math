Ladder 21 — Perimeter, Circumference & Irregular Figures

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape. For a circle we call it circumference. For irregular figures, break the shape into familiar pieces — but only count edges that are on the boundary. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

Perimeter & Circumference Formulas
ShapeFormulaVariables
SquareP = 4ss = side length
RectangleP = 2l + 2wl = length, w = width
TriangleP = a + b + ca, b, c = three side lengths
Regular polygonP = n · sn = number of sides, s = side length
Circle (circumference)C = πd = 2πrd = diameter, r = radius
Semicircle (arc only)arc = πrHalf the full circumference
Quarter-circle (arc only)arc = πr / 2One quarter of full circumference
💡
Exact vs. approximate. Leave π in your answer for the exact value (e.g. 20π ft). Substitute π ≈ 3.14 only when asked for an approximation.
⚠️
Irregular figures: outside edges only. Interior cuts you made to split the figure are NOT part of the perimeter. Trace the outer boundary and add only those segments.
📐
Missing sides. On L-shaped or notched figures, unlabeled sides can often be found using the opposite labeled sides — opposite parallel sides must account for the same total span.
