Ladder 24 — Surface Area of Cylinders, Cones & Spheres

Curved solids have faces you can't unfold into flat polygons — but you can still roll them flat. The lateral surface of a cylinder unrolls into a rectangle; a cone's lateral surface unrolls into a sector. Master each formula here and earn a badge 4 in a row.

Surface Area Formulas — Curved Solids
SolidTotal Surface AreaLateral (side only)
Cylinder SA = 2πr² + 2πrh Lateral = 2πrh  (rectangle when unrolled: 2πr × h)
Cone SA = πr² + πrl Lateral = πrl  (l = slant height)
Sphere SA = 4πr² No lateral/base split — the whole surface is curved
Key Vocabulary & Relationships
TermMeaning / Formula
r (radius)Distance from center of circular base to edge
d (diameter)d = 2r; if given diameter, divide by 2 first
h (height)Perpendicular distance between the two bases (cylinder) or from base to apex (cone)
l (slant height)Cone: distance along the lateral surface from base edge to apex. l = √(r² + h²)
π≈ 3.14 (or leave exact as π)
💡
Cylinder unroll trick. The lateral face of a cylinder unrolls into a rectangle whose width is the circumference (2πr) and whose height is h. So lateral SA = 2πr × h = 2πrh.
⚠️
Slant height ≠ vertical height. For a cone, the formula uses slant height l, not h. If you're given h and r, compute l = √(r² + h²) first.
📐
Open vs. closed. A can with no lid omits one circular base (−πr²). A cone with no base uses only the lateral surface (πrl). Know which faces are present before applying the formula.
