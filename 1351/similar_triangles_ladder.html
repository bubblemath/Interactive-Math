Similar Triangles

Master the three similarity shortcuts — AA, SSS~, and SAS~ — set up and solve proportions to find missing sides, find scale factors, and apply similarity to real-world problems.

Similarity Shortcuts
NameWhat you needNotes
AA Angle · Angle Two pairs of congruent angles — the third pair is automatic (angle sum = 180°)
SSS~ Side · Side · Side (ratios) All three pairs of corresponding sides are in the same ratio (proportional)
SAS~ Side · Angle · Side (ratios) Two pairs of sides in the same ratio AND the included angle is congruent
Key Concepts
ConceptDefinition / Formula
Similar (∼)Same shape, possibly different size — angles equal, sides proportional
Scale factork = (side of image) ÷ (corresponding side of original)
Proportiona/b = c/d → cross-multiply: ad = bc
CASTCCorresponding Angles of Similar Triangles are Congruent
CSSTPCorresponding Sides of Similar Triangles are Proportional
Similarity vs ≅Similar = same shape; Congruent = same shape AND same size
💡
AA is the easiest test: If you can show any two pairs of angles are equal, the triangles are similar — you never need to check sides. The third pair of angles must also match because all three sum to 180°.
⚠️
SSS~ checks ratios, not lengths: The sides do not have to be equal — they must form the same ratio. Check that a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ before concluding SSS~.
📐
SAS~ needs the included angle: The congruent angle must be between the two proportional sides — exactly like SAS for congruence. If the angle is not included, SAS~ does not apply.
