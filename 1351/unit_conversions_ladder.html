Ladder 20 — Unit Conversions & Dimensional Analysis

A unit fraction equals 1 — it just wears different clothes. Multiply by the right unit fractions in a chain and the unwanted units cancel, leaving exactly the unit you need. Master U.S. customary facts, metric prefixes, and the chain-multiplication technique. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

💡
Cancel units like factors. Write the starting value × (unit fraction). Any unit that appears in both numerator and denominator cancels. Arrange fractions so the unit you want ends up on top.
⚠️
Flip the fraction to go the other way. 12 in / 1 ft converts feet → inches. 1 ft / 12 in converts inches → feet. Choosing the wrong orientation multiplies by the reciprocal — a very common mistake.
📐
Metric shortcut. Moving one prefix step left multiplies by 10; one step right divides by 10. km → m is three steps right, so multiply by 1,000. The prefix chart is your map.
