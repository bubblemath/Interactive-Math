Mean, Median, Mode & Midrange

Ten rungs from computing each measure to choosing the right one for a given context — and understanding what a single outlier does to all four.

Mean (x̄)
x̄ = Σx ÷ n
Sum all values, divide by count. Sensitive to outliers.
Median
Middle value (sorted)
Odd n → middle item. Even n → mean of two middle items. Resistant to outliers.
Mode
Most frequent value
Can be none, one, or several. Only measure for categorical data.
Midrange
(max + min) ÷ 2
Midpoint between extremes. Very sensitive to outliers.
⚠️
Median for even n: sort the data, then average the two middle values. The median is NOT always a value in the data set.
📌
Mode can be "none": if every value appears exactly once, there is no mode — not zero, just no mode.
💡
Outlier rule of thumb: Mean and midrange are pulled toward the outlier. Median moves slightly. Mode is unaffected.
