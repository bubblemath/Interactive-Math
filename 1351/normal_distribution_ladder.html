Normal Distribution & Percentiles

Ten rungs from recognizing normal-curve properties and applying the Empirical Rule, to computing z-scores, reading percentiles, and solving real-world normal problems.

💡
z-score formula: z = (x − μ) / σ. A positive z means x is above the mean; negative means below. z = 0 is exactly at the mean.
⚠️
Empirical Rule applies only to bell-shaped distributions. Skewed or bimodal data do not follow the 68–95–99.7 pattern.
📌
Percentile from z: z = −1 → ≈ 16th percentile; z = 0 → 50th; z = +1 → ≈ 84th; z = +2 → ≈ 97.5th; z = +3 → ≈ 99.85th.
