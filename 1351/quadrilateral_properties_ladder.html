Quadrilateral Properties

Identify and classify the seven special quadrilaterals, recall their key properties, navigate the hierarchy (square ⊂ rhombus ⊂ parallelogram), and apply properties to find missing angles and sides.

Special Quadrilaterals — Properties
ShapeKey Properties
Trapezoid At least one pair of parallel sides; consecutive angles between parallel sides are supplementary (sum to 180°)
Isosceles Trapezoid Base angles are congruent; non-parallel legs are congruent
Kite Two pairs of adjacent congruent sides; diagonals are perpendicular; longer diagonal bisects shorter diagonal and bisects one pair of opposite angles
Parallelogram Opposite sides parallel and congruent; opposite angles congruent; consecutive angles supplementary; diagonals bisect each other
Rhombus All parallelogram properties + all 4 sides congruent; diagonals perpendicular to each other; each diagonal bisects opposite angles
Rectangle All parallelogram properties + 4 right angles; diagonals congruent and bisect each other
Square All rectangle + all rhombus properties; 4 congruent sides, 4 right angles, perpendicular congruent diagonals
Hierarchy (Subset Relationships)
StatementWhy
Square ⊂ RectangleA square has 4 right angles, so it satisfies the rectangle definition
Square ⊂ RhombusA square has 4 congruent sides, so it satisfies the rhombus definition
Rectangle ⊂ ParallelogramA rectangle has two pairs of parallel sides
Rhombus ⊂ ParallelogramA rhombus has two pairs of parallel sides
Parallelogram ⊂ TrapezoidA parallelogram has at least one pair of parallel sides
All angles sum to 360°Every quadrilateral, regardless of type
💡
Parallelogram key rule: Opposite angles are equal; consecutive angles are supplementary. If one angle is known, you can find all four: x, 180°−x, x, 180°−x.
⚠️
Rhombus vs. Square: A rhombus has 4 equal sides but angles are NOT required to be 90°. A square is the special rhombus where all angles are also 90°.
📐
Diagonal properties matter: Parallelogram diagonals bisect each other (midpoint); rhombus diagonals are also perpendicular; rectangle diagonals are also congruent.
