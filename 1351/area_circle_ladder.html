Ladder 13 — Area of a Circle

Master the area formula for circles, sectors, and composite figures. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

Formula Reference
FormulaEquationNotes
Area (radius)A = πr²r = radius; square first, then multiply by π
Area (diameter)A = π(d/2)² = πd²/4Substitute r = d/2 into A = πr²
Sector AreaA = (θ/360°) · πr²θ = central angle in degrees; sector is that fraction of the full circle
Annulus AreaA = π(R² − r²)R = outer radius, r = inner radius; ring between two circles
💡
Square the radius first: A = π·r², not A = (πr)². Squaring before multiplying by π is the most common order-of-operations mistake here.
⚠️
Area vs. Circumference: Area = πr² (units²); Circumference = 2πr (units). Area grows with the square of r — doubling r quadruples the area.
📐
Leave in terms of π unless told to approximate. Write 36π cm² not 113.1 cm².
