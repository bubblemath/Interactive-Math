Count choices at each step, then multiply.

Ten rungs — two-step choices, multi-step problems, digit restrictions, and real-world scenarios. Earn a badge on each rung by getting 4 in a row.

📐 The Rule
Total = n₁ × n₂ × … × nₖ
nᵢ = number of choices available at step i.
Multiply across ALL steps.
🌳 Tree Diagram Check
branches at level 1 × branches at level 2 …
Each path from root to leaf
is one complete outcome.
⚠ Restrictions
Fill restricted positions first
Restrictions reduce choices at one step.
Handle them before the free positions.

How it works

01 / List every step

Break the task into its independent decisions. Each slot, course, digit, or choice is one step.

02 / Count options at each step

Write the number of available choices for that step alone, ignoring the other steps.

03 / Multiply

Total outcomes = product of all step counts. If any step has 0 options, the total is 0.

04 / Handle restrictions first

When a step has a constraint (must be even, can't repeat, etc.), fill that slot first so you know exactly how many choices remain for the others.

⚠️

Most common mistake: Adding instead of multiplying. If you have 3 shirt choices AND 4 pant choices, the total outfits are 3 × 4 = 12, not 3 + 4 = 7. Addition counts "one or the other" — multiplication counts "both together."
