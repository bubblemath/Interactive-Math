Undefined Terms & Basic Notation

Ten rungs from identifying the six undefined terms of geometry, through naming conventions and symbolic notation, to reading angles and applying notation in figures.

Quick Reference — Undefined Terms & Notation
TermWhat it isNotation example
PointA location; dimensionless (no size)A
LineInfinite points in both directions; 1-D or ℓ
Line SegmentTwo endpoints and all points between
RayOne endpoint, extends infinitely in one direction (A = endpoint)
PlaneFlat, infinite 2-D surfacePlane P or plane ABC
AngleTwo rays sharing a vertex (common endpoint)∠ABC, ∠B, or ∠1
💡
Why "undefined"? Points, lines, and planes are called undefined because geometry uses them as foundational building blocks — they are described, not formally defined using simpler terms.
⚠️
Ray direction matters. Ray AB (→ from A through B) and Ray BA (→ from B through A) are different rays — the first letter is always the endpoint.
📌
Angle naming: ∠ABC means B is the vertex. The middle letter of a three-letter angle name is always the vertex.
