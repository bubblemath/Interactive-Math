💡
Euler's Formula: For any convex polyhedron, V − E + F = 2. Use it to find a missing count when two of the three are known.
⚠️
Prisms vs Pyramids: A prism has two congruent parallel polygon bases; a pyramid has one polygon base and triangular lateral faces meeting at an apex. Cones and cylinders are not polyhedra — their faces include curves.
📐
Cross-sections: Slicing a 3D solid parallel to its base often reveals the base shape. A horizontal cut through a cone gives a circle; a diagonal cut can give an ellipse, parabola, or hyperbola.
