Ladder 14 — Compass & Straightedge Constructions

Master Euclid's tools: what each construction builds, the steps involved, and the geometric reasoning that makes each one work. Earn a badge on each rung by answering 4 in a row correctly.

Construction Reference
ConstructionTools UsedKey Idea
Congruent SegmentCompass + straightedgeLock compass to length AB; transfer that arc to a new ray
Congruent AngleCompass + straightedgeCopy the arc and chord-length that define the angle's spread
Perpendicular BisectorCompass + straightedgeTwo equal-radius circles from A and B form a rhombus; its diagonals are ⊥ and bisect each other
Angle BisectorCompass + straightedgeEqual arcs from the vertex form a rhombus; its diagonal bisects the angle
Congruent TriangleCompass + straightedgeCopy side, copy side, copy side (SSS) — three lengths fix the triangle
Isosceles TriangleCompass + straightedgeTwo equal-radius arcs from each base endpoint meet at the apex
Equilateral TriangleCompass + straightedgeBoth arcs use radius = side length; all three sides are automatically equal
💡
Compass = fixed radius. Every arc drawn without adjusting the compass has the same radius. That locked radius is how equal lengths are transferred.
⚠️
No measuring allowed. A straightedge draws lines but has no markings. You cannot use ruler markings or a protractor — only arcs and straight lines.
📐
Rhombus shortcut: Both the perpendicular bisector and the angle bisector secretly construct a rhombus. Rhombus diagonals are always perpendicular and always bisect each other.
