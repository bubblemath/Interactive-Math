Guest Notes · what this floor tests

Basic rules
Equally likely outcomes: P = favorable ÷ total. P(not A) = 1 − P(A). P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B).
Venn diagrams
Each number counts one region only. A set’s total adds every region inside its circle. “And” is the overlap; “or” is everything in either circle, counted once.
At least one
P(at least one) = 1 − P(none) = 1 − (1 − p)ⁿ for independent trials.
Expected value
E(X) = Σ x·P(x), using net winnings (subtract the cost of playing). A negative value means players lose money on average.
Binomial
n fixed trials, two outcomes, constant p, independent: X ~ B(n, p). “Less than k” is P(X ≤ k − 1); “at most k” is P(X ≤ k); “between a and b” includes both.
