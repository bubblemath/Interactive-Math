Cross-check the records.

Two files, one question: do they really differ? Some records come in pairs (the same person, before and after); some are two separate groups. Get the design right, keep Group 1 first, and let the two-sample applet do the arithmetic.

Case Notes · what this room tests

Independent or matched?
Matched pairs: each value has a natural partner (the same person measured twice, twins, the same item at two stores). Independent: two separate groups.
Which tab?
Counts out of totals → Two-Sample Proportions (pooled p̂). Two separate groups with means → Independent Means (df rounded down). Paired data → Dependent Means (Matched Pairs), which computes d = Group 1 − Group 2.
Hypotheses
Independent: H0: μ₁ = μ₂ (or p₁ = p₂), Ha: μ₁ <, >, or ≠ μ₂. Matched pairs: H0: μd = 0, Ha: μd <, >, or ≠ 0, with the direction matching how d is defined.
Group order
Whatever is Group 1 in the claim goes first in the applet. If you reverse the order, the sign of the test statistic flips and you must read the other tail.
Two-way tables
For two proportions, successes are one column: x₁ out of n₁ in row 1, x₂ out of n₂ in row 2.
