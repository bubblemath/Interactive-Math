The Hollowmere Hotel · Final exam

Five haunted floors.
One way out.

You checked in to the Hollowmere for one night. That was a long time ago. Each floor holds five locks, and each lock opens on one piece of the course, from collecting data to hypothesis tests. Open all five and the floor gives you a five-digit code; key it in to reach the next floor. Clear all five floors to check out. Every visit deals a fresh set of problems, so you can wander the halls as many times as you like.

House rules

  • Each floor carries its own Guest Notes: the rules and formulas for exactly what that floor tests.
  • Floors 2 to 5 have the course applets built in, the same ones we use in class and on the final.
  • Every lock has a hint that tells you the strategy without giving the number.
  • Some locks want several answers; all must be right before the lock turns. A box outlined in red is the one to look at again. Give probabilities to 4 decimal places unless the lock says otherwise.
  • Short on time? Start with the floor you feel least sure about; each floor works on its own.
  • Wrong answers cost you nothing but time. There is no timer and no penalty.
