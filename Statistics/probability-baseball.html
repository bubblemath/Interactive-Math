Assign the eleven dice sums to five outcomes, play three innings, then find out what the sample space knew all along.

Round 1: make your lineup card

Two dice, eleven possible sums. Give every sum a job. You have exactly five outs, two singles, two doubles, one triple, and one home run to hand out — no sum gets left blank, and no sum gets two jobs.

Assignment card

Three innings. Three outs an inning, or twelve batters, whichever comes first. Runners move up one base on a single, two on a double, three on a triple, and everybody scores on a home run.
