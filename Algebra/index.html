Every lesson turns on one decision.

Fourteen self-contained applets, each isolating the single decision its lesson depends on and drilling it until it is automatic — the sign before the digits, the operation before the procedure, the setup before the solving. Not more worked examples: practice at the exact moment a lesson asks you to choose. Pick a ladder below.

01 · format

Eleven rungs, three arcs

Each ladder climbs from recognising the decision to making it under pressure. Start on any rung; nothing is locked.

02 · the bar

Four in a row earns a badge

Answer with the mouse or press 1–4. A wrong answer resets the streak — the target is fluency, not eventual correctness.

03 · the point

The decision, not the arithmetic

Many rungs refuse to compute at all — they ask which move, which sign, which form. Each ladder also flags the classic mistake it exists to kill.
