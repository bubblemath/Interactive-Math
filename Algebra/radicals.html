Parts of a radical

The radical is the √ symbol. The radicand is under it. The index is the small number; if none is shown, it is 2 (square root).

Key rule for simplifying:

A radical is simplified when the radicand has no perfect-square factor other than 1.

1 · Break it into squares

Pick a number. Watch it split into equal perfect squares.

Perfect squares that divide it
