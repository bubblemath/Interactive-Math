How they work

Ten rungs, four in a row

Every ladder opens with the idea and the mistake it causes, then ten rungs of practice. Questions are generated fresh each time, so a ladder can be climbed repeatedly. Answer with the mouse or the number keys 1–4; press Enter for the next question. Progress lives in this browser only — it is not sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data will clear it.
