Case Notes · what this room tests

Magic squares
Every row, column and both diagonals share one total. Find a complete line to get the total, then subtract the two known entries on any line through the gap.
Arithmetic sequences
A constant common difference d. The nth term is a₁ + (n − 1)d. Two adjacent known terms hand you d directly.
Union and intersection
A ∪ B is everything in either set, each listed once. A ∩ B is only what they share. With three sets, the triple intersection must be in all three.
Base five
Four places are 5³ 5² 5¹ 5⁰ = 125, 25, 5, 1. Multiply each digit by its place value and add. Digits only run 0–4.
Properties of real numbers
Order swapped → commutative. Only brackets moved → associative. Factor spread over a sum → distributive. Unchanged by 0 or 1 → identity. Landed on 0 or 1 → inverse.
