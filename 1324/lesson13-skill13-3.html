Skill 13.3: Union Rule & Odds

Goal: use the union rule, recognize mutually exclusive events, and convert between odds and probability.

Questions to explore

Load spade or king. Which card is green, and why subtract it?

The king of spades is in both events, so it would be counted twice: 13 + 4 − 1 = 16 cards.

Load red 7 or black 8. Why is nothing green?

No card is both a red 7 and a black 8: the events are mutually exclusive, so just add 2 + 2.

Load the two-dice examples. Which outcomes are green?

(2, 4) and (2, 5) for the first; (6, 5) for the second. That gives 15/36 = 5/12 and 7/36.

Odds of 5 to 3: why is the probability 5/8 and not 5/3?

5 to 3 means 5 chances for and 3 against: 8 chances in all.
