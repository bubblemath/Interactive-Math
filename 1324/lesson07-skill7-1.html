Skill 7.1: Setting Up LP Problems

Goal: define decision variables, organize a word problem in a table, and write its constraints and objective function.

Organization table builder

Read the story, fill in every cell, and press Check my table. Then reveal the constraints and objective that the table gives.

From table to problem

Each row of the table becomes one constraint. The last row becomes the objective function.

Questions to explore

In the canoe problem, why is the cost row an inequality with ≤ and not =?

You can spend less than $4,800; you just cannot spend more. “Budget” and “holds up to” both mean ≤.

In the animal-feed problem, why are the nutrient rows ≥?

They are minimum requirements: at least 30 g of protein. More is allowed; less is not.

The feed problem has two contracts that are not rows of the table. How do you write them?

“At least 2 units of B” is y ≥ 2. “B no more than 3 times A” is y ≤ 3x (test: with 2 units of A, B can be at most 6).

Why do we always add x ≥ 0 and y ≥ 0?

You cannot buy or make a negative number of items.

Worked examples

Pick a type. Try each step yourself, then reveal it. Press New example as often as you like.
