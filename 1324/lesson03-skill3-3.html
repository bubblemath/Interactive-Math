    Questions to explore

    In the h preset, evaluate x = 1. Which piece does it use, and why does the graph have two circles there?

    x = 1 satisfies x ≥ 1, so h(1) = −1 + 5 = 4 (closed circle at (1, 4)). The first piece only approaches (1, 3), so that circle is open.

    Change the first piece of h to x ≤ 1 (so both pieces include 1). What goes wrong?

    Now x = 1 has two outputs, 3 and 4, so h is no longer a function. The domain check flags the overlap.

    In the phone plan, why is the second piece 25 + 7(x − 10) and not 7x?

    You always pay the $25, and the $7 applies only to the gigabytes over 10. That is x − 10 of them.

    Use the |x| preset. How is the absolute value function piecewise?

    |x| = −x when x < 0 and x when x ≥ 0. Each piece is a line, and they meet at (0, 0).
