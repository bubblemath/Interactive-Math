Questions to explore

Load the two coins. Why is P(1 head) twice P(0 heads)?

Two outcomes (ht and th) give 1 head, but only tt gives 0.

Load the broken monitors. Which count is most likely, and why does x stop at 3?

x = 1 (28/55). There are only 3 broken monitors, so a sample can't contain more than 3.

Load the sum of two dice. What shape is the histogram?

A symmetric “tent” peaking at 7, the sum with the most pairs (6 of 36).

Load “One missing.” What must the blank be?

1 − 0.7 = 0.3.
