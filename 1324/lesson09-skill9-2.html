Questions to explore

In Example 3, compare the two corner tables. What do you notice?

The minimum of w (48 at (4, 1)) equals the maximum of z (48 at (2, 3)).

Run Example 3 to the end. Where do 4 and 1 appear in the final tableau?

In the bottom row under s₁ and s₂: exactly y₁ = 4 and y₂ = 1.

In the dog breeder problem, are the answers whole numbers? Does that make sense?

No: 3/5 and 9/5 bags a day. Averaged over several days (3 bags of feed 1 and 9 of feed 2 every 5 days) it is a sensible plan.

Load Three constraints. Why is there no graph of the dual?

The dual has three variables (one per constraint), so its region is three-dimensional. Simplex still solves it.
